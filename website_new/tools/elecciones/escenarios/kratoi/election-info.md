**Election information for the [[Kratoi|Republic of Kratoi]]**: the political profile of each of its 23 provinces, grouped by department, and the position of every presidential candidate and party at every election from 4025 to 4105.

> [!warning] Provisional
> This is a working design document, not settled canon. All scores are best-guess interpretations of the lore. Populations come from the 4000 province data (`data/start_4000/provinces/kratoi_provinces.json`). Deputies assume the proposed 240-seat Congress of Deputies elected in single-member districts, about one per 35,000 inhabitants. The elvish protectorate provinces (Ardaphiel, Sylmar, Naeril, Eldrenwood) are outside the republic's electorate and are not listed. Losing candidates the lore never names are listed as *Unnamed*.

# How to read the scores

Every axis runs from **−10** (the first-named pole, fully) to **+10** (the second-named pole, fully). **0** is neutral or evenly split.

Province scores describe the **baseline electorate of the founding generation (c. 4030–4050)**. Later drift is noted per department. Candidate scores describe the platform each candidate actually ran on in that election.

# Provinces by department

## Argent Coast

*Population 2,140,000 · 5 provinces · 62 deputies*

The western seaboard and the republic's commercial face: banks, trading houses, fishing fleets and the ports that feed the Hesperian sea lanes. Politically it is the great swing department: its merchant elite bankrolls the Federalists while its docks and fishing towns lean Nationalist, and elections here are usually decided by whether trade or tariffs are on voters' minds.

- **Argostana** (700,000; Kratian; coast; 20 deputies). The richest province in Kratoi and the seat of its trading houses and banks. Its merchant class is the Federalists' principal backer; cosmopolitan, free-trading, and allergic to anything that frightens foreign capital.
- **Vireuil** (420,000; Kratian; coast; 12 deputies). Harbour towns, fishing fleets and the salting and smoking yards. Sailors and dockhands who have felt Ustilian tariffs and naval harassment directly; reliably Nationalist on the Ustilian question, more pragmatic on everything else.
- **Saumance** (380,000; Kratian; plains; 11 deputies). Market-garden and grain country that feeds the coastal cities. Moderate, prosperous and unexcitable; the province that most often votes for the eventual winner.
- **Clairval** (340,000; Kratian; plains; 10 deputies). A province of commuter villages and small mills strung along the roads to Argostana, increasingly absorbed into the coast's economy. Liberal-leaning and mildly Nationalist.
- **Belmoux** (300,000; Kratian; hills; 9 deputies). The coast's poorer hinterland: hill farms and fading market towns that see little of Argostana's money. More traditional and more receptive to anti-elite appeals than the rest of the department.

## Kratoi Centrale

*Population 2,100,000 · 5 provinces · 59 deputies*

The heart of the republic: the capital, its universities, the first garrisons stormed in 4023, and the plains that supplied the revolutionary army. The Nationalist heartland: unionist, secular, anti-Ustilian and confident in the institutions it built.

- **Rimas** (600,000; Kratian; plains; 17 deputies). The capital: a university town turned seat of government, with its cafés, free press and the Palace of Congress. The most progressive, secular and institution-minded electorate in the country.
- **Dorvanne** (420,000; Kratian; plains; 12 deputies). Wealthy farmland and market towns around the capital, home to many revolutionary veterans and their families. Patriotic, reform-minded and reliably Nationalist.
- **Ferrolles** (390,000; Kratian; plains; 11 deputies). Kratoi's foundry province: ironworks, and later the chi-foundries that arm the military. A working-class, pro-industry electorate that likes a strong state and a strong army.
- **Austaine** (360,000; Kratian; hills; 10 deputies). Hill country on the department's edge, where the capital's influence thins out. Nationalist on the national question, but more conservative in its habits.
- **Montrivel** (330,000; Kratian; hills; 9 deputies). Quiet uplands of orchards, small towns and retired officials. Moderate, tidy-minded and mildly protective of its landscape.

## Haut-Kratoi

*Population 1,268,000 · 4 provinces · 36 deputies*

The northern march against Mazarin: hills, mountains, and three provinces that are Mazarine by culture. Poor, devout and security-conscious since the Mazarine sneak attack through Alascur in 4001 (when Fileon's relief force saved the garrison, a debt the north has never quite forgotten). It resents Rimas's centralism and its refusal to give its minority any formal recognition.

- **Vosgard** (360,000; Kratian; hills; 10 deputies). The department's Kratian-majority province and its administrative hub. Hawkish and fond of public works, and more inclined than its Mazarine neighbours to want the minority assimilated.
- **Kessling** (330,000; Mazarine; hills; 9 deputies). Mazarine-speaking hill farms and wooden churches. Wants cultural recognition and local self-rule, subsidies for its farmers, and as little of Rimas as possible.
- **Drommel** (308,000; Mazarine; mountains; 9 deputies). Mountain province holding the **Alascur Pass** (folded in here, about 8,000 people), where the 4001 invasion struck. The most security-obsessed electorate in Kratoi, and the least hostile to Ustilus.
- **Nordhaven** (270,000; Mazarine; hills; 8 deputies). The smallest and most remote Mazarine province: isolated, tradition-bound, and deeply suspicious of every government it has ever had.

## Bas-Kratoi

*Population 1,740,000 · 5 provinces · 50 deputies*

The southern march facing Ustilus: hill vineyards, two Ustilian-majority provinces, and the families who kept their commercial ties to the Empire through the revolution. The Federalist core: decentralist, traditional, religiously observant, and convinced that confronting Ustilus is suicide.

- **Sarvaux** (380,000; Kratian; hills; 11 deputies). Kratian hill farms and vineyards on the frontier. Conservative and pragmatic, with an independent streak that keeps it competitive.
- **Denvire** (360,000; Ustilian; forest; 10 deputies). Ustilian-majority forest province where Feynism and the Church of the Inheritor both have deep roots. The most pro-Ustilian electorate in the republic.
- **Aubrit** (340,000; Kratian; hills; 10 deputies). Landowner country of old provincial families turned gentleman farmers. The Federalists' social backbone.
- **Castelnoir** (360,000; Ustilian; hills; 10 deputies). The wealthier of the two Ustilian provinces, with cross-border merchants and wine exporters who live off trade with the Empire. Pro-Ustilian, free-trading and institution-minded.
- **Miravel** (300,000; Kratian; plains; 9 deputies). Southern grain plains with a large tenant-farmer class. Traditional, anti-elite, and quick to vote against whoever is in power in Rimas.

## Forêt Orientale

*Population 1,160,000 · 4 provinces · 33 deputies*

The eastern forests bordering the elvish communes: lumber, druids and Spirit Faith, and the province that suffered worst from Fileon's inquisitors. Fiercely anti-Ustilian and protective of personal liberty, but intensely localist and distrustful of Rimas. Torn between the logging economy and its own forests.

- **Sylvenne** (320,000; Kratian; forest; 9 deputies). Forest towns on the elvish border, long used to living alongside the communes. The most pro-elf and most environmentalist electorate in the country.
- **Haudric** (300,000; Kratian; forest; 9 deputies). Logging province and the old Lumber Guild's heartland, later the Lumber Consortium's. Wants jobs and timber contracts, and resents the elves and the conservationists.
- **Verchamps** (280,000; Kratian; forest; 8 deputies). Clearings, villages and Spirit Faith shrines in deep forest. Libertarian, devout and anti-government in roughly equal measure.
- **Lisieroy** (260,000; Kratian; forest; 7 deputies). The smallest and wildest province, where the druids still hold real authority. The most federalist and the most environmentalist electorate in Kratoi.

## Drift over the republic's life

- **Argent Coast**: turns sharply pro-industry and pro-Ustilian-trade in the Schkreld boom (4065–4079), then swings back after the Crisis of '86 wipes out its investors.
- **Kratoi Centrale**: stays the Nationalist core until 4090. In the SPM era it splits: the capital's professionals hold to institutionalism while Ferrolles's foundry workers go populist, and some go Crimson by 4105.
- **Haut-Kratoi**: moves toward the SPM in the 4090s, drawn by pensions and farm subsidies. It is also the first to panic during the Mazarine civil war (4103 onward), when its security score climbs toward +8.
- **Bas-Kratoi**: becomes the Restoration Alliance's stronghold after 4100. The alleged Crimson attacks on farms near the Ustilian frontier push its security and pro-Ustilian scores toward their extremes by 4105.
- **Forêt Orientale**: stays split between logging and conservation, swinging toward the Nationalists after the 4079 revelations about the massacre of elves and Judith's Natural Conservation Law, and away again whenever timber prices fall.

# Open questions

- The losing candidates of most elections are unnamed in the lore, as are the opposition's name in 4030–4040 and the two minor candidates of 4105.
- Canonical places from the Kratoi page (Carpentia, Grevara, Soucart, Bruchart, Roumnet) are not tied to any province in the data, so this document does not place them.
- The electoral system (single-member districts with a second round, plus a proposed Senate) is a proposal awaiting a ruling.
