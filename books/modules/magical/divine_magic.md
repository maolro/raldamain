# Divine Magic
**Category**: Magical  
**Primary Stat**: Wisdom  
**Counter**: Undead, demons, corrupted entities — divine power inflicts bonus damage and conditions  
**Countered By**: Divine-resistant entities; fights that go too long to recover miracle charges

## Overview
Divine Magic is the blessing-and-smiting rank. It calls upon divine power to protect the innocent, punish the guilty, and reshape the battlefield through holy or sacred force. Unlike other magical ranks, it doesn't scale by raw damage — it scales by *consecration*, a persistent state that amplifies everything done within it.

The mechanic is **Miracles and Consecrated Ground**. You have a pool of Miracle charges (3, resetting on Long Rest). Some abilities cost a Miracle. Consecrated Ground is a zone you can create — allies in it fight better, undead in it take penalties, and certain abilities can only be used in it. Managing the zone and your Miracle pool is the main decision loop.

## Core Passive — Holy Presence
You and allies within close range deal **+4 damage** against undead, demons, or other supernaturally evil entities (GM defines this category). Additionally, you can sense the presence of strong evil or divine objects within medium range without any action.

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Smite** *(1 action)*  
Channel divine wrath through your next attack. That attack deals **+15 bonus damage**. Against undead, demons, or enemies carrying powerful curses, this instead deals **+25 bonus damage**. Costs 1 Miracle.

**Consecrate** *(1 action)*  
Designate a small area (3×3 spaces) as Consecrated Ground. The zone lasts for 3 rounds. Allies in the zone gain +2 threshold. Undead or demonic enemies in the zone take 1 direct Hit at the start of each of their turns.

**Divine Shield** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: you or an ally within range would take Hits. Create a burst of divine light — reduce the Hits taken by 1 (minimum 0). Usable twice per encounter (before Miracles).

---

## Tier II — Adept

**Curse** *(1 action)*  
Lay a divine curse upon one enemy. For 3 rounds: the target deals -8 damage on all attacks and has disadvantage on Willpower checks. Costs 1 Miracle. Only one Curse active at a time; applying a new one ends the previous.

**Summon Guardian** *(2 actions)*  
Call forth a divine guardian spirit. It occupies 1 space adjacent to you and has 3 Hits, threshold 12. Each round, you may spend 1 action to direct it to attack (12 damage) or defend (grants +3 threshold to one adjacent ally for 1 round). The guardian disappears if it reaches 6 Hits, or after 4 rounds. Costs 1 Miracle.

**Blessing of Fortitude** *(1 action)*  
Place a lasting blessing on one ally. For the next 2 rounds, that ally's threshold is +5 and they are immune to fear effects. The blessing can be "cashed in" early as a reaction to reduce incoming Hits by 2 (ends the blessing if used this way).

**Radiant Wave** *(2 actions)*  
A burst of holy energy in a medium AoE. Deals 18 damage to all enemies within it. Undead and demonic enemies take an additional 1 direct Hit. Costs 0 Miracles but a 2-round cooldown applies.

---

## Tier III — Master

**Divine Intervention** *(3 actions)*  
Call for direct intervention from your divine patron. Effect depends on the situation (work with the GM), but mechanically: remove all Hits from one ally (full restoration), or deal 3 direct Hits to one enemy (divine judgment), or create a 5×5 Consecrated Ground that lasts the rest of the encounter. Costs 2 Miracles. Once per Long Rest.

**Grand Consecration** *(1 action)*  
Expand any active Consecrated Ground to cover the entire visible battlefield. Lasts 5 rounds. All undead and demonic enemies are *Sundered* — their threshold is halved while in it. Costs 1 Miracle.

**Avatar of Divinity** *(passive upgrade)*  
Your Miracle pool increases from 3 to 5. Your Holy Presence bonus increases from +4 to +8 damage. When you are inside Consecrated Ground, you may use Smite without spending a Miracle (once per round).

---

## Design Notes
- Divine Magic's biggest strategic decision is Consecrate placement. Smart GMs will move enemies out of the zone; smart Divine casters will force enemies to walk through it.
- *Divine Intervention* is intentionally vague — it should feel like a miraculous rescue that the GM and player design together. Mechanically, the three options (heal/nuke/consecrate) cover the three likely emergencies.
- Consecrate + Summon Guardian + Blessing creates a strong defensive bubble. The counterplay is AoE that doesn't care about positioning.
- Pairs naturally with Divine Warrior (Mixed), which uses divine blessings in physical combat.
