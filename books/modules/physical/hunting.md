# Hunting
**Category**: Physical  
**Primary Stat**: Intelligence or Perception (player's choice at Tier I)  
**Counter**: Any enemy with high threshold — Hunting reduces it to nothing  
**Countered By**: Enemies that move unpredictably or have anti-analysis abilities (Illusion Magic)

## Overview
Hunting is the intelligence rank — it wins fights before they happen. Where other ranks improve what you do in a fight, Hunting improves what you know. A skilled Hunter knows the enemy's threshold, their patterns, their weaknesses, and the terrain better than the enemy does.

The core mechanic is **Analysis** and **Threshold Reduction**. Hunting abilities reduce the target's effective threshold for the whole party's benefit, expose specific vulnerabilities, and set up ambushes that guarantee advantageous combat openings. Hunters excel in scouting phases (before combat), persistence fights (they get better as they gather more information about an enemy), and boss fights (one high-value target to fully dissect).

They deal low damage directly and have poor survivability. Their value is multiplying the effectiveness of every other character in the group.

## Core Passive — Predator's Eye
Once per turn, when you observe an enemy take damage or make an attack roll, you may note their pattern (this happens automatically — no action required). After observing an enemy **3 times**, you have fully **Analyzed** them (you know their threshold, their most dangerous ability, and their current Hit total).

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Identify Weakness** *(1 action)*  
Target one enemy you can see. Their effective threshold is reduced by **4** until the start of your next turn (for all attacks from all sources). This reduction does not stack with itself but stacks with other threshold-reduction effects.

**Terrain Mastery** *(free action, once per encounter)*  
Survey the battlefield. For the rest of the encounter, you ignore all difficult terrain and have advantage on any roll involving the environment (climbing, hiding, ambushing from a specific point, etc.).

**Hunter's Mark** *(1 action)*  
Mark one target. While marked, you deal +5 damage to that target. If you are hidden when you mark them, the first attack against a marked target has advantage. Only one Mark active at a time; switching targets costs 1 free action.

---

## Tier II — Adept

**Expose** *(2 actions)*  
Fully analyze a target, reducing their threshold by **8** (for all sources) for 2 rounds. This is more powerful than Identify Weakness and lasts longer but costs more. The target is also marked automatically.

**Ambush Protocol** *(passive)*  
When combat begins and you have scouted the encounter beforehand (GM confirmation), you and all allies gain a free action on the first round. Additionally, one enemy of your choice begins the encounter with 1 Hit already applied (you found their weakness before the fight).

**Tracking Strike** *(1 action)*  
An attack that deals 14 damage but more importantly reveals the target's current Hit total and threshold to you and all allies. This information is accurate until the target uses a healing ability.

**Pinpoint** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: an ally targets an enemy you have Analyzed. You may call out a vulnerability — that ally's attack deals +6 bonus damage. Once per round.

---

## Tier III — Master

**Ruin** *(2 actions, requires fully Analyzed target)*  
Exploit your complete knowledge of the enemy. Reduce their threshold by **15** until the end of the encounter and deal **1 direct Hit** (threshold bypass). This is the ultimate vulnerability exposure — used right before a party member's big-damage ability.

**Phantom Blind** *(1 action)*  
Misdirect a target by exploiting their attack patterns. The target's next attack automatically misses (no roll). Usable against enemies you have observed attacking at least once. Once per enemy per encounter.

**Alpha Predator** *(passive upgrade)*  
Your Predator's Eye passive now fully Analyzes enemies after **1 observation** instead of 3. Additionally, all threshold reductions you apply are increased by **+3** (Identify Weakness becomes -7, Expose becomes -11). Hunting now operates at full speed from the first round.

---

## Design Notes
- Hunting's power is entirely party-dependent in multiplayer. Solo, it's a decent skirmisher. In a group, the threshold reductions multiply every other character's output.
- *Ruin* combined with a Colossus's *World Breaker* or Assassin's *Assassination* is the designed combo: lower threshold to near zero, then dump maximum damage for maximum Hits.
- The Analysis requirement (3 observations for Tier I) is intentional pacing — it prevents Ruin from going off round 1. Ambush Protocol bypasses this for prepared fights.
- Countered by Illusion Magic clones (which one is the real target?) and anything with anti-analysis text.
