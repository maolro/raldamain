# Presence
**Category**: Physical  
**Primary Stat**: Charisma  
**Counter**: Enemies with morale mechanics — you can terrify them out of acting  
**Countered By**: Mindless enemies (immune to morale), enemies that attack the Presence character directly

## Overview
Presence is the command rank. It changes how fights feel — enemies hesitate, allies fight harder, and the battlefield psychology shifts in your favor. A high-Presence fighter is not necessarily the one dealing the most damage; they're the reason everyone else is dealing more.

The mechanic is **Morale**. Enemies have a Morale value (usually 3–5). When Morale hits 0, the enemy breaks — they attempt to flee, hesitate, or act suboptimally. Presence abilities reduce enemy Morale and raise ally morale (which functions as a mechanical bonus). Mindless enemies (automatons, undead, constructs) are immune to Morale effects.

The Presence fighter is targeted by smart enemies first because of their force-multiplication. Pairing with Fortitude or Reflexes to survive is strongly recommended.

## Core Passive — Commanding Aura
Allies within medium range of you deal **+3 damage** on all attacks. Additionally, you always know the current Morale of any enemy you can see (the GM tells you when it changes).

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Inspire** *(1 action)*  
Grant one ally an immediate free attack (they make it now, outside their turn, as if it were a reaction). This attack uses their normal attack bonus. Usable once per round.

**Intimidate** *(1 action)*  
One enemy within medium range must make a Willpower check (DC 11). On failure, reduce their Morale by 1 and they cannot target you with their next action (they flinch away from the direct confrontation). On success, no effect.

**Rally** *(1 action)*  
Call to an injured ally within range. They remove 1 Hit. Usable once per Short Rest per ally (each ally can benefit once between rests).

---

## Tier II — Adept

**War Cry** *(2 actions)*  
A thunderous battle shout. All enemies within medium range must make Willpower checks (DC 13) or lose 1 Morale. Enemies who were already at 1 Morale are immediately **Shaken** (disadvantage on all rolls until they recover Morale). All allies in range gain advantage on their next attack.

**Fear Aura** *(1 action, Concentration)*  
Maintain an oppressive presence for up to 3 rounds. While active, any enemy who moves adjacent to you must make a Willpower check (DC 12) or spend their movement retreating instead of advancing. They are not compelled — they choose to retreat, but the compulsion costs no action.

**Bolster** *(1 action)*  
Target one ally. For 2 rounds, they gain +3 to their threshold (they fight better knowing you have their back). They also have advantage on their next defense roll.

**Shatter Resolve** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: an enemy deals Hits to you or an ally. Call out that enemy's cowardice or weakness. They must make a Willpower check (DC 12) or reduce their Morale by 2 immediately. Once per round.

---

## Tier III — Master

**Demoralize** *(3 actions)*  
A devastating display of dominance. All enemies in the encounter must make Willpower checks (DC 16) or reduce their Morale to 1 immediately (not by 1 — it drops to 1 regardless of current value). Enemies already at 1 Morale are immediately **Routed** (spend their next 2 turns fleeing). Mindless enemies unaffected. Once per encounter.

**Hero's Presence** *(1 action)*  
Choose one ally. For the next round, that ally's threshold is doubled and they deal +10 damage on all attacks. The effect is dramatic and visible — this feels like the hero's moment. Once per encounter per ally.

**Unbreakable Leader** *(passive upgrade)*  
Your Commanding Aura bonus increases from +3 to **+6** damage. Additionally, allies within range of you cannot have their Morale reduced by enemy abilities (they are immune to enemy morale attacks while near you). You can maintain **two Concentration abilities** simultaneously instead of one.

---

## Design Notes
- Presence is strongest in multi-enemy encounters where Morale applies to multiple targets simultaneously.
- *Demoralize* is a potential fight-ender against mortal enemies, but requires 3 actions and once-per-encounter — save it for critical moments.
- The direct targeting vulnerability is intentional: enemies in a real fight would focus the commander. GMs should play intelligent enemies this way.
- Pairs extremely well with Hunting (which exposes weaknesses) — together they make every other character in the party significantly more effective.
