# Fortitude
**Category**: Physical  
**Primary Stat**: Constitution  
**Counter**: Enemies relying on single-hit damage — their hits land below threshold and deal 0  
**Countered By**: Direct Hit abilities, multi-hit pressure, status effects that bypass threshold

## Overview
Fortitude is the purest expression of the Hit system's design space — it directly manipulates the threshold number that determines whether damage matters. A Fortitude character forces enemies to completely rethink their attack patterns: what was previously a 1-Hit strike becomes a 0-Hit graze.

The rank has no offensive abilities of its own. It exists to make a character a wall — alive when everyone else is down, letting allies operate safely. Its weakness is that direct Hit abilities, which bypass threshold entirely, still work normally. Smart opponents (or ones with Hunting) will identify the Fortitude fighter and route around them.

## Core Passive — Iron Body
Your base Hit Threshold is **+8** (18 instead of 10). Additionally, the first time per encounter you would take a Hit, that Hit is reduced by 1 (minimum 0). This reduction resets at the start of each Short or Long Rest.

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Brace** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: you are targeted by any attack. Plant your feet and tighten your stance. Increase your threshold by **+5** until the end of this attack only. Stack with Iron Body passive.

**Endure Pain** *(free action, once per round)*  
Activate when you have taken at least 1 Hit this encounter. Ignore the mechanical effects of your current Hit total for 1 round (you still have the Hits, but penalties that would apply at 3+, 4+, etc. Hits do not apply this round). At the end of the round, all effects return.

**Thick Skin** *(passive)*  
Any attack dealing exactly 1 Hit against you deals 0 Hits instead, once per round. You still track the attempt (it counts as an attack for retaliation purposes), but the Hit is negated. Usable 1 time per round.

---

## Tier II — Adept

**Steel Yourself** *(1 action)*  
Concentrate your fortitude into a prepared defense. Until your next turn, your threshold is **doubled** (base 36 with all passives active). You cannot move while Steel Yourself is active. This is extremely powerful but requires you to commit your position.

**Shake Off** *(1 action)*  
Remove 1 Hit you have taken. Alternatively, remove 1 Mark or status effect applied to you. Usable once per Short Rest.

**Unyielding** *(passive)*  
When you take 2+ Hits from a single attack, reduce the Hits taken by 1 (minimum 1). This stacks with Iron Body's first-hit reduction — a single devastating attack effectively loses 2 Hits against you.

**Punishing Defense** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: an attack against you deals 0 Hits (due to threshold). You may immediately make a free melee counterattack dealing 14 damage. The attacker cannot defend against this counterattack (no defense roll).

---

## Tier III — Master

**Living Fortress** *(1 action)*  
Enter a defensive stance lasting until the start of your next turn. While active:
- Your threshold increases by **+15** (stacking with all other bonuses)
- Allies within adjacent spaces gain **+5 threshold** as well
- You cannot attack, but you may use *Brace* and *Punishing Defense* unlimited times

**Unbreakable Will** *(passive)*  
When you would take your 6th Hit (Defeat condition), instead take only 5 Hits. This effect triggers once per encounter. You survive at 5 Hits, standing. On the turn this triggers, gain 1 free action.

**Pinnacle of Iron** *(passive upgrade)*  
Your Iron Body threshold bonus increases from +8 to **+12** (base threshold 22). Additionally, *Thick Skin* now triggers twice per round instead of once. Your threshold is now effectively too high for most standard attacks to deal any Hits at all without special abilities.

---

## Design Notes
- Fortitude is the hardest rank to attack once fully built. The counterplay must exist in other mechanics: direct Hits, threshold reduction (Hunting), AoE that bypasses positioning.
- *Punishing Defense* creates an interesting decision for attackers — attacking a Fortitude character and dealing 0 Hits means giving them a free hit back.
- The combination of Iron Body + Unyielding + Brace means a single-target attacker effectively loses up to 3 Hits from a big swing. This is the intended design for multi-rank characters.
- Consider capping total threshold at 30–35 in your playtesting to prevent completely unattackable characters.
