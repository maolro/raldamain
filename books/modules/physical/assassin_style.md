# Assassin Style
**Category**: Physical  
**Primary Stat**: Dexterity  
**Counter**: High-threshold enemies (Fortitude, Colossus) — individual hits don't pile up enough Hits  
**Countered By**: Low-evasion, low-threshold targets; any enemy in a poor position

## Overview
Assassins don't win fights — they end them before they start. The rank rewards patience, positioning, and information: knowing the enemy's weaknesses, getting into the right position, and then detonating a setup for massive burst damage.

The core mechanic is the **Mark** system. Most Assassin abilities build up Marks on a target, and payoff abilities deal dramatically increased damage or direct Hits when a Mark is present. This means Assassins are deceptively weak without setup and explosively powerful with it.

Against high-threshold enemies, the Assassin's small hits deal 0 Hits each. The solution is to build Mark stacks, then use a high-damage finisher to blow through the threshold in one instance. Against evasive enemies, the Assassin has no guaranteed-hit tools at Tier I — they rely on landing the setup hits first.

## Core Passive — Shadow Step
Once per round, when you move, you may treat one space you pass through as **difficult terrain for enemies only** (you leave behind environmental interference — smoke, caltrops, a trip wire). Additionally, attacks you make while **Hidden** automatically hit (no defense check) but consume the Hidden status.

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Quick Strike** *(1 action)*  
A fast but light attack. Deal 12 damage. If this deals a Hit, apply 1 **Mark** to the target. Marks last until the start of the target's next turn (or until consumed).

**Expose** *(1 action)*  
Study a target's defenses without attacking. Apply 1 **Mark**. Until the Mark is consumed or expires, the target's effective threshold is reduced by **3** against your attacks only.

**Vanish** *(1 action)*  
Enter the **Hidden** state. You cannot attack while hidden, but enemies cannot target you directly. Hidden breaks if you enter the same space as an enemy, use an ability with a visible effect, or at the start of your next turn. You may move up to your full movement speed as part of this action.

---

## Tier II — Adept

**Marked for Death** *(2 actions)*  
A precise strike aimed at a vital point. Deal 20 damage. For each Mark on the target, deal **+8 bonus damage**. Consumes all Marks. If total damage dealt 3+ Hits, the target is also **bleeding** (1 direct Hit at the start of each of their turns for 2 turns).

**Shadowstep** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: you are targeted by an attack. Teleport to any adjacent space before the attack resolves. The attack misses automatically if the new position is outside its range or arc. Usable once per round.

**Preparation** *(1 action)*  
Set up a trap or ambush position in your current space. The next enemy who enters or passes through this space must make a Dexterity check (DC 13) or take 15 damage and 1 **Mark**. Traps last until triggered or until 2 rounds pass. You may have 2 active traps at once.

**Death from Above** *(2 actions, requires elevation or Hidden)*  
Drop onto or ambush a target from an unexpected angle. Deal 28 damage. If used from Hidden, this deals **+1 direct Hit** regardless of threshold. Immediately apply 1 Mark to the target.

---

## Tier III — Master

**Assassination** *(3 actions, requires 3+ Marks on target)*  
Consume all Marks. Deal damage equal to **15 × number of Marks consumed**. This damage ignores the target's threshold entirely (counts as direct Hits). This is a single-target nuke intended to end fights against priority targets.

**Phantom Rush** *(2 actions)*  
Blur through up to 3 enemies in a line, dealing 15 damage to each and applying 1 Mark to each. This movement does not trigger reactions from the targets hit. End your turn in any space along the path.

**Shroud** *(passive upgrade)*  
You no longer lose Hidden at the start of your turn — you remain hidden until you attack or are found. Additionally, Quick Strike no longer breaks Hidden when used (you may make one Quick Strike per Hidden state without revealing yourself). Vanish may now be used as a **reaction** once per encounter.

---

## Design Notes
- Marks are the central resource — protect them carefully, as enemies can remove marks through certain abilities (Fortitude's *Shake Off*, some Presence auras).
- The Assassin has almost no AoE. In multi-enemy encounters they must prioritize targets and be careful about action economy.
- *Assassination* is the payoff ability. 3 Marks × 15 = 45 damage. This requires 3 prior setup actions — the cost is real.
- Pair well with Hunting (which can Expose weaknesses) or Mind Magic (which can paralyze targets in place for easier setup).
