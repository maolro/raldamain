# Unleashed Mind
**Category**: Physical  
**Primary Stat**: Intelligence  
**Counter**: Enemies without mental defenses — psychic damage bypasses threshold  
**Countered By**: Mindless enemies; losing Sanity too fast to maintain coherent ability use

## Overview
Unleashed Mind is the most dangerous rank in the system — dangerous to the enemy, and dangerous to the user. It represents a character who has partially severed the mental limiters on their psychic potential, channeling raw mental force through sheer cognitive violence. The power is real and extreme. The cost is Sanity.

The core mechanic is the **Sanity Track**. You start each encounter with 5 Sanity. Certain Unleashed Mind abilities cost Sanity. If your Sanity reaches 0, you enter the **Unleashed** state — you lose control of your targeting (GM may redirect some of your attacks toward random targets, including allies) but your abilities become dramatically stronger. Sanity recovers on Long Rest; Short Rest recovers 1.

This is the rank for players who like power with a price attached.

## Core Passive — Psychic Threshold Break
Your psychic attacks deal **direct Hits** (they bypass the threshold entirely) instead of threshold-based damage. However, psychic attacks have no physical component — they cannot affect mindless enemies (constructs, most undead, entities without a mind).

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Psychic Spike** *(1 action)*  
Fling a bolt of mental force at one target. Deal **1 direct Hit**. Costs 0 Sanity. This is your standard attack — weak but consistent.

**Mind Lens** *(1 action)*  
Read the surface thoughts of one target in range. You learn their next intended action this round and may interrupt it (they lose 1 action this turn). Costs 1 Sanity.

**Mental Veil** *(1 action)*  
Wrap your mind in a psychic barrier. Until your next turn, you are immune to mind-reading, confusion, and fear effects. Allies within close range gain advantage on Willpower checks. Costs 0 Sanity.

---

## Tier II — Adept

**Psychic Crush** *(2 actions)*  
A focused mental assault. Deal **2 direct Hits** to one target. The target also loses 1 action on their next turn (the mental pain disrupts their thinking). Costs 1 Sanity.

**Fractured Reality** *(2 actions)*  
Assault the mental model of reality held by all enemies in a small area. Each affected enemy must make an Intelligence check (DC 13) or be **Confused** for 1 round (they target a random character with their next action, including allies). Costs 2 Sanity.

**Mind Over Body** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: you would take a physical Hit. Intercept it with mental force — halve the Hits taken (round down). You still feel the attack but your mind softens it. Costs 1 Sanity.

**Awakened** *(passive)*  
When your Sanity is at 2 or lower, you deal +1 to all direct Hits caused by your abilities. At Sanity 0 (Unleashed), all direct Hits deal +2 instead. The closer to the edge, the stronger you get.

---

## Tier III — Master

**Total Annihilation** *(3 actions)*  
Unleash everything in a psychic nova. Deal **3 direct Hits** to all enemies in a medium range. Costs 3 Sanity. If you have 0 Sanity (Unleashed), this instead deals **4 direct Hits** to all targets and the Unleashed redirect applies (some may target allies).

**Ego Collapse** *(2 actions)*  
Destroy the mental coherence of one target. The target must make an Intelligence check (DC 15) or lose the ability to use any abilities requiring Concentration for 2 rounds, and their Morale drops to 0 immediately. Costs 2 Sanity. Against mindless enemies, this does nothing.

**Absolute Awakening** *(passive upgrade)*  
Your Sanity Track increases to 7. Abilities that cost Sanity now cost 1 less (minimum 0), except Total Annihilation which still costs 3. Additionally, when Unleashed (0 Sanity), you gain 1 extra action per round — the loss of sanity accelerates your mind to terrifying speed, even as it loses precision.

---

## Design Notes
- The Unleashed state is a GM tool as much as a player mechanic. Define in session 0 how much control the GM takes: some tables prefer "suggest a redirect" while others want it fully random.
- *Awakened* creates deliberate tension: experienced Unleashed Mind players will spend Sanity strategically to lower it to 2 for the bonus without hitting 0.
- Mindless enemy immunity is the core weakness. A dungeon full of constructs should feel threatening for this rank.
- Pairs well with Presence (Presence's morale pressure + Unleashed Mind's direct Hits = two completely different attack vectors that cover each other's weaknesses).
