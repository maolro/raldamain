# Reflexes
**Category**: Physical  
**Primary Stat**: Dexterity  
**Counter**: Slow, predictable single-hit attackers (Colossus) — you can sidestep every swing  
**Countered By**: AoE attacks you can't dodge individually; abilities that don't require attack rolls

## Overview
Reflexes is the evasion rank — not about taking fewer Hits, but about not getting hit at all. Where Fortitude shrugs off what lands, Reflexes ensures nothing lands. The core fantasy is the untouchable fighter: a blur on the battlefield who reacts to everything, repositions constantly, and makes attackers feel like they're fighting smoke.

The mechanic is **extra reactions**. Standard characters get 2 reactions per round. Reflexes fighters get more, and their reactions are more powerful — they can dodge, reposition, reroll, or interrupt enemy turns. In a system where 2 reactions feel limiting, Reflexes removes that limitation.

The weakness is clear: you can dodge things that require a defense check. You cannot dodge direct Hit abilities, certain spells, or effects that say "no defense check". And you are fragile — your threshold is unchanged, so if something does land, it hurts.

## Core Passive — Combat Instincts
You gain **1 additional Reaction** per round (3 total). Additionally, once per round when you use a reaction to dodge, you may move up to 1 space as part of the dodge (the movement does not cost a reaction).

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Sidestep** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: you are targeted by an attack. Make a Defense check with advantage. On success, the attack misses entirely (no damage). On failure, the attack still hits but you move 1 space away from the attacker (repositioning).

**Spring Away** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: any enemy moves adjacent to you. Immediately move up to 2 spaces away. This movement does not provoke reactions from the triggering enemy. You may use this once per round.

**Flicker** *(1 action)*  
Move up to 3 spaces. This movement ignores difficult terrain and does not provoke reactions from any enemy. Gain advantage on your next Defense check this round.

---

## Tier II — Adept

**Redirect** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: you would take 1+ Hits from an attack. Instead of taking the Hits, immediately move to any adjacent space and the Hits are reduced by 1. The attack "grazes" rather than lands clean. Usable once per round.

**Anticipate** *(free action, once per round)*  
Spend 1 action at the end of your turn to prepare. Until your next turn, the first attack against you automatically fails (you move before it completes). You cannot use this and Sidestep in response to the same attack.

**Momentum** *(passive)*  
When you use your movement and Flicker in the same turn, your next attack this turn deals +8 bonus damage (you build kinetic energy through movement).

**Counter-Movement** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: an enemy uses a movement ability to reposition themselves. You may immediately move up to your full movement speed. This does not cost your normal reaction — it is a special interrupt tied to enemy movement only.

---

## Tier III — Master

**Phantom Reflex** *(reaction)*  
Trigger: any attack, effect, or ability would affect you. This is the most powerful dodge — it works against any targeted effect including spells, unless the ability explicitly states "no dodge" or "no defense check". Make a Defense check at +4. On success, the effect misses or passes through you entirely. Once per encounter.

**Flow State** *(1 action)*  
Enter a heightened reflexive state lasting 3 rounds. While active:
- All your Defense checks are made at advantage
- You gain 1 additional reaction (now 4 total per round with the passive)
- At the start of each of your turns, move up to 1 space for free

**Ghost Step** *(passive upgrade)*  
You now gain **2 additional reactions** per round (4 total, or 5 with Flow State). Additionally, your movement never provokes reactions from enemies, regardless of circumstances. You cannot be flanked.

---

## Design Notes
- Reflexes is the most satisfying rank to play against Colossus (big slow attacks) and the least useful against Illusion Magic or effects without attack rolls.
- *Phantom Reflex* is an "oh no you don't" ability — it should be rare and powerful. Its once-per-encounter limit is critical; don't remove it.
- The extra reactions are the rank's currency. Abilities that drain reactions (certain enemy effects: "target must use both reactions or take double damage") are natural hard counters.
- At full build (Tier III + Flow State), 4+ reactions per round means this fighter almost never takes a Hit from standard attacks. This is fine — the threshold is base 10, so the rare hit that does land still hurts.
