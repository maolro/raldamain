# Colossus Style
**Category**: Physical  
**Primary Stat**: Strength  
**Counter**: High-evasion builds (Reflexes, Assassin) — they can sidestep attacks before they land  
**Countered By**: Persistence builds; multiple weak fast attackers (chip damage bypasses nothing since Colossus has high threshold, but it adds up)

## Overview
Colossus fighters are weapons of mass destruction in slow motion. Every swing is an event: it reshapes terrain, sends enemies flying, and forces every nearby fighter to react. The rank specializes in heavy weapons (greataxes, mauls, war picks) and punishes anyone who stands still or groups up.

The core tension is **commitment vs payoff**. Colossus abilities cost 2–3 actions and telegraph themselves, giving fast enemies a window to escape or dodge. Against targets that can't move quickly — armored enemies, casters in the backline, enemies stuck in chokepoints — Colossus is devastating.

The passive threshold boost means weak attacks are completely wasted against a Colossus fighter. Smart enemies must pool resources or use abilities that deal direct Hits to pierce the defense.

## Core Passive — Immovable
Your Hit Threshold is **+5** (base 15 instead of 10). You cannot be moved, pushed, or knocked prone by any effect of Tier I or lower unless you choose to be.

---

## Tier I — Initiate

**Cleave** *(2 actions)*  
Swing in a wide arc, targeting all enemies in a cone in front of you. Deal 20 damage to each. Enemies hit must succeed a Strength check (DC 12) or be knocked back 1 space.

**Overhead Smash** *(2 actions)*  
A slow, telegraphed blow aimed at one target. Deal 25 damage. If the target fails to dodge (defense check vs your attack), they are also **knocked prone** until the start of their next turn (they have disadvantage on their defense rolls while prone).

**Stone Wall Stance** *(1 action, free to exit)*  
Plant your feet. Until the start of your next turn: you cannot move, but your threshold increases by **+10** (to 25 if passive is active) and you have advantage on all defense checks. Taking a reaction does not break the stance.

---

## Tier II — Adept

**Earthquake Slam** *(3 actions)*  
You drive your weapon or fist into the ground. All enemies within a medium radius take 30 damage and must succeed a Dexterity check (DC 14) or fall prone. Creates **difficult terrain** in the affected area until cleared.

**Titan's Throw** *(2 actions)*  
Hurl your weapon (or a large object) at a target up to far range. Deal 30 damage. On hit, the projectile embeds itself — the target has disadvantage on movement until they spend 1 action removing it. You can recall your weapon as a free action on your next turn.

**Seismic Step** *(2 actions)*  
Move up to your full movement speed in a straight line, ignoring all difficult terrain. Each enemy whose space you pass adjacent to takes 15 damage. Targets at the end of your movement take 20 damage and are pushed back 2 spaces.

**Unbreakable** *(reaction)*  
When you would take a Hit, you may activate this. Roll a d6. On 4+, reduce the Hits taken by 1 (minimum 0). On a failure you still take full Hits but gain **1 Rage Token** (usable as part of future Cleave or Overhead Smash to add +5 damage). You may have up to 3 Rage Tokens at once.

---

## Tier III — Master

**World Breaker** *(3 actions)*  
Your most powerful strike. Choose one target. Deal **45 damage**. If this deals 3 or more Hits, every other enemy within close range also takes 20 damage from the shockwave. Regardless of Hits dealt, the target is **staggered** (loses 1 action on their next turn).

**Colossus's Roar** *(1 action)*  
You bellow a war cry that shakes the battlefield. All enemies within medium range must make a Willpower check (DC 15) or be **shaken** (−2 to attack rolls for 1 round). Allies within range gain advantage on their next attack. Cooldown: 1 round.

**Immovable Object** *(passive upgrade)*  
Your threshold increases by a further **+5** (total +10, base 20). Additionally, you may use *Stone Wall Stance* as a **free action** once per round instead of spending 1 action.

---

## Design Notes
- Colossus forces enemies to choose: engage at melee range and risk massive AoE, or stay far away and potentially be out of range of other allies.
- The threshold boost is its primary defensive tool; debuff-heavy builds (Hunting, Mind Magic) can strip it or ignore it by lowering enemy thresholds instead.
- *Unbreakable* creates a feedback loop: taking hits rewards aggression. Don't over-tune the Rage Token bonus.
- World Breaker is the signature ability — it should feel like a genuine battlefield event, not just a damage roll.
